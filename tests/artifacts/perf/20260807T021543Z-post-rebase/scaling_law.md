# Scaling assessment

No scaling law is inferred from this run. The 20 Dumbnet observations repeat
one fixed input to measure variance; they do not vary a natural size axis. The
single hoon-138 observation establishes feasibility, exact parity, wall time,
and peak RSS only.

A future scaling experiment should select inputs with measured noun count,
source size, and dependency count, then collect at least five sizes with three
or more repetitions each. Until then, statements about linear, superlinear, or
asymptotic behavior would be unsupported.
